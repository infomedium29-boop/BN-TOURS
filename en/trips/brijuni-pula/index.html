About this destination

An island escape and a Roman city

Brijuni lies just off the coast, yet feels a world away. A boat from Fažana takes visitors to Veliki Brijun, with its walking paths, meadows and traces of history. Leave some time for Pula’s Arena and old streets afterwards.

How your trip could look

A sample outline for your group. The final itinerary depends on departure point, date and group preferences.

Suggested route

The Brijuni islands

Veliki Brijun is known for peaceful paths, natural scenery and archaeological remains. Visits depend on the national park’s boat schedule and visitor rules.

Suggested route

An afternoon in Pula

The Arena, Forum and historic city gates make a natural continuation to a day on the island. The mix of landscape and city life suits groups with different interests.

Suggested route

Free time and return

Leave time for Pula’s waterfront or old streets before returning.
