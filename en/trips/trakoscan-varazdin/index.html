About the destination

A lakeside castle and baroque streets

Trakošćan and Varaždin make a relaxed but rewarding combination. There is time for the castle and its lake before heading to Varaždin’s elegant historic centre.

Request a quote ↗
What to see

Trakošćan Castle

The castle rises above a lake, with attractive grounds. Allow time for an outdoor walk and, when available, the museum.

Varaždin

Palaces, squares and the Old Town are a fitting end to the day.
