About the destination

Seaside promenades and a lively city

Opatija is known for its seaside promenade and old villas, while Rijeka has an energetic city feel. Together they show two very different sides of the Kvarner region.

Request a quote ↗
What to see

The Opatija promenade

Lungomare runs along coastal parks, hotels and open sea views. The walking distance can be adjusted for the group.

Rijeka

Korzo, the old town and Trsat make interesting additions if time allows.
