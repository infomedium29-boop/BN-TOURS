About this destination

A city worth exploring on foot

The best way to discover Dubrovnik is without rushing: Stradun, quiet stone lanes, city walls and the old harbour. For a group visit, planning enough time for the highlights makes all the difference.

How your trip could look

How your trip could look

A sample outline for your group. The final itinerary depends on departure point, date and group preferences.

Suggested route

The old town

Stradun, Orlando’s Column, historic churches and the streets inside the walls form the heart of a visit.

Suggested route

Views and time to explore

Depending on the group, an itinerary could include the city walls or free time near the waterfront. Entrance tickets are arranged separately.

Free time and return

Choose one part of the city for free time instead of rushing through everything.
