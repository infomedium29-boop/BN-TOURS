About the destination

Two seaside towns with character

Rovinj is best explored on foot: winding streets climb towards wide sea views. Poreč offers a different pace and the remarkable Euphrasian Basilica.

Request a quote ↗
What to see

Old Rovinj

Packed stone houses, small squares and St Euphemia Church make the old town worth exploring slowly.

Poreč

Alongside its lively waterfront, Poreč is known for the UNESCO-listed Euphrasian Basilica.
